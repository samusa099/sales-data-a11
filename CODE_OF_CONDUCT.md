# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The **Sales Data Analysis — Assessment 11** project is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, professional, and learning-focused environment for everyone who participates.

All contributors, reviewers, users, and maintainers are expected to interact in good faith and treat one another with dignity, regardless of experience level, background, identity, location, profession, or technical ability.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- provide constructive, specific, and actionable feedback;
- remain patient with learners and first-time contributors;
- focus criticism on the work rather than the individual;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them transparently;
- respect data privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property;
- document assumptions and limitations honestly; and
- support an environment where questions and responsible experimentation are welcome.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- insulting, demeaning, threatening, or deliberately disruptive communication;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sharing credentials, confidential company records, personal data, or sensitive screenshots;
- knowingly submitting malicious formulas, unsafe connections, manipulated files, or misleading analysis;
- plagiarism, false attribution, or presenting unverified findings as established facts;
- repeated spam, off-topic promotion, or attempts to derail project discussions; and
- retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Project-Specific Standards

Because this repository contains Excel and Power BI files, contributors must also:

- use synthetic, anonymised, or properly authorised data;
- avoid embedding passwords, tokens, gateway credentials, or connection strings;
- disclose external links, queries, custom visuals, and data-source dependencies;
- preserve the documented folder structure unless a change has been agreed;
- validate dashboard totals against the source data before submission; and
- clearly distinguish designed previews from actual report screenshots.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and repository comments;
- project-related communication in public or private channels;
- submitted datasets, dashboards, documentation, and visual assets; and
- situations where a participant is representing this project or its community.

## Reporting a Concern

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a GitHub issue with only the information appropriate for public discussion.

For concerns involving personal information, credentials, confidential data, security risks, or sensitive evidence, **do not post the details publicly**. Follow the private reporting guidance in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

A useful report should identify:

1. what happened;
2. where and when it occurred;
3. the relevant issue, pull request, comment, or file;
4. any non-sensitive supporting evidence; and
5. the outcome or assistance being requested.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may take proportionate action, including:

- requesting clarification or correction;
- editing or removing inappropriate content;
- closing issues or pull requests;
- rejecting a contribution;
- restricting participation; or
- reporting serious platform-policy violations to GitHub.

Enforcement decisions will consider the severity, context, impact, repetition, and willingness to correct the behaviour.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted for the needs of an educational Excel and Power BI analytics repository and follows widely accepted open-source community principles.
